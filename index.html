GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Is my appointment confirmed after I submit?

No. It starts as a pending request. The clinic secretary must confirm your schedule. You can check the status in the patient portal.

Can I book if I’m a first-time patient?

Yes. Start with your basic details and the service you need. The clinic will guide you on anything else needed for your first visit.

How much will my visit cost?

Fees depend on the consultation, scan, or procedure. Ask the clinic to confirm the applicable fee before your visit. This prototype does not collect payments.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

You can request a schedule change through the patient portal. The new schedule remains pending until the clinic confirms it.

This appointment form is not monitored for emergencies. For urgent concerns, seek care at the nearest emergency department.
